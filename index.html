Architectural story

Designed around the view.

Aurelia House is organized as a sequence of quiet transitions—from the shaded arrival court to the open living pavilion, from private garden rooms to the water's edge. Every space is positioned to frame light, landscape, and horizon.

Amenities

Considered, and complete.

  • Infinity pool

    Eighteen metres of still water, set flush to the horizon line.

  • Private garden

    Olive, rosemary and tall grasses, kept deliberately quiet.

  • Water-facing living room

    A double height pavilion that opens fully across its southern face.

  • Five ensuite bedrooms

    Each with its own terrace or garden aspect, and its own bathroom.

  • Wellness room

    Sauna, cold plunge and a treatment room opening to a courtyard.

  • Secure two-car garage

    Arrival court with gated entry, and storage for a third vehicle.

Lifestyle & location

Close to everything.
Apart from the ordinary.

The residence sits on a quiet south-facing waterfront plot, screened from the coast road by mature planting. Marinas, restaurants and the old town are a short drive; the international school and the airport sit comfortably within the same half hour. The address is deliberately understated, and the outlook across the water will not be built out.
